What 4.7 changes
  • GEMV workgroup is 256 threads, 8 columns per subgroup, every lane walks K. On Metal (subgroup 32) that is 64 columns per workgroup. 4.6 sg4 spent 256 threads on 4 columns, and on K=3136 most of those lanes had no block.
  • MLP stays f16 per block with an f32 sum, relu2 fused into the up projection, same policy as 4.6.
  • Mamba SSD is one thread per (head, dim) with vec4 state updates. 4.6 launched 7680 barrier reductions per layer.
  • The K split follows the device subgroup width. Metal is 32 lanes. The bench asks for that when the adapter allows it.
